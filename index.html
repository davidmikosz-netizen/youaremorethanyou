You Are Not Just You

Micro-dispatches exploring internal ecology, the gut-brain axis, and what happens when we stop treating our bodies as machines and start treating them as habitats.

Episode 01 3:15 min

You Are a Habitat: The Hidden Conversation Between Your Gut and How You Feel

Stop thinking of your body as a machine to fuel, and start seeing it as a living landscape. An exploration of the trillions of microbes inside your digestive tract—and how their daily chemical chatter directly shapes your mood, energy, and mental clarity.

  • The vagus nerve: the high-speed two-way communication line between gut and brain.
  • How gut microbes produce mood-regulating neurochemicals like serotonin and GABA.
  • The bridge to Multitudes: mapping the internal voices and inputs that make up "you."

Episode 02 2:50 min

Your Body Is a Meadow: Why Your Internal Landscape Craves Wild Variety

Farms that only plant one thing are brittle; meadows with diversity endure.The American Gut Project showed why dietary species diversity—aiming for 30 distinct plants weekly—builds resilience inside your colon.

  • Why fiber is not monolithic: distinct plant species feed specialized bacterial niches.
  • How frequently eating the same processed foods reduces your gut diversity.
  • How to track botanical variety without calorie counts or obsession.

Episode 03 3:40 min

Your Body Is an Ancient Forest: How to Reseed a Devastated Ecosystem

An ancient forest doesn't regenerate from a single packet of flower seeds, and a devastated gut won't recover with just a store-bought probiotic. How and why recovering your gut's biome after a colonoscopy, antibiotics, or stomach bug can bring your internal landscape back to life.

  • Why store-bought probiotic pills are just transient tourists, not permanent residents.
  • How broad-spectrum antibiotics can accidentally clear-cut your internal landscape.
  • How whole-ecosystem restoration (FMT) re-seeds biological networks from the ground up.
